It sits around the notch, not in it

The notch is part of the hardware, so no app can draw inside it. NotchWave draws a black shape that continues the notch’s own outline: flat against the top of the screen, with the same rounded lower corners. At rest it lines up exactly with the real notch and disappears. When sound starts it widens on a spring and smooth glass waveforms rise from flat lines, and when the sound stops it settles back after a short pause so it doesn’t flicker between songs.

The size comes from macOS itself (the screen’s safe-area inset and the two usable menu bar areas either side), so it fits every notched MacBook. On a display without a notch it grows a virtual one out of nothing while audio plays.

From the vinyl to the notch

This follows on from my Max/MSP and Jitter rotating-vinyl visualiser, which put low frequencies at the centre of the record and highs at the rim. NotchWave keeps that mapping and unrolls it along the menu bar: bass sits next to the notch and treble reaches out to the tips, mirrored on both sides.

Three stages, all on your Mac

  1. 1 · Capture

    Hear the Mac

    AVAudioEngine for the microphone. ScreenCaptureKit for everything the Mac plays, or one app on its own.

  2. 2 · Analyse

    Split the sound

    A 2048-point FFT with Apple’s Accelerate framework, about 94 times a second, folded into 24 log-spaced bands.

  3. 3 · Draw

    Move like macOS

    A SwiftUI Canvas redrawn every display frame, with spring physics for the shape and smooth line waveforms with a frosted-glass fill.

Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Audio is analysed in memory and thrown away a few milliseconds later.

Run it on your Mac

Needs
A Mac on macOS 14 Sonoma or later, and Xcode 15 or later (or its command line tools).
Works on
Every notched MacBook Air and MacBook Pro. Other displays get a virtual notch.
Permissions
Microphone, and Screen & System Audio Recording for computer or app audio. macOS asks the first time.
git clone https://github.com/atharvabankarsound/notch-audio-visualizer.git
cd notch-audio-visualizer
./scripts/build-app.sh
open build/NotchWave.app

A waveform icon appears in the menu bar. Use it to switch between All Computer Audio, Microphone or a single app, change the style, or quit. To work on the code, open Package.swift in Xcode and press Run.
